A little about us

Good baking starts with slowing down.

Butter & Crumb began with a tiny oven, a well-loved recipe book, and a belief that the best things are made with patience. Today, we still make our dough slowly, choose ingredients thoughtfully, and bake in small batches.

No shortcuts, no fuss. Just familiar flavours, a few seasonal surprises, and something warm to share.
